## 📜 A Secret Message

Two ancient regions lie beyond the main road through Codoria. They are not required to complete the main adventure, but teams with enough courage and Kingdom Points may choose to unlock them.

These bonus missions review skills from Unit 1 and give teams an opportunity to earn additional points.

## 🗺️ Bonus Region Map

| Region | Knowledge required | Mission type | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 🏕️ Training Grounds | JavaScript fundamentals | ⭐ Bonus Region | 🔐 Available to Unlock |
| 🏛️ Hall of Elements | DOM and events | ⭐ Bonus Region | 🔐 Available to Unlock |

## 🔑 Unlock Rules

- A team spends **10 Kingdom Points** to unlock one region.
- Completing the main mission returns the **10 points**.
- Completing the bonus quest earns **5 additional points**.
- If the team does not finish, it can still earn **3 points** by explaining its attempts and discoveries.
- Teams may unlock the regions in either order.

## 👥 Royal Roles

Complete each mission in teams of three:

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| ⚔️ Knight Coder | Writes the code |
| 🧭 Royal Navigator | Reads the mission and guides the team |
| 🔍 Bug Hunter | Tests the code and investigates errors |

Rotate roles before entering the second bonus region.
